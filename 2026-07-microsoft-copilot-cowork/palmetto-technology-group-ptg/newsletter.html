Microsoft Copilot Cowork: Your AI That Gets Work Done

What if you could hand off a complex, multi-step project to a reliable colleague and get back a finished deliverable — not just a rough draft? That's exactly what Microsoft's latest innovation makes possible for your business.

In June 2026, Microsoft officially launched Copilot Cowork as generally available worldwide, making it accessible to all Microsoft 365 Copilot tenants. Unlike the chat-based Copilot you may already be using to summarize emails or generate ideas, Cowork is a fully agentic system — one that plans, executes, and delivers real work from start to finish. You describe the outcome you want, and Cowork handles the rest.

Powered by Work IQ, Cowork automatically draws on data from your Outlook, Teams, Excel, and other Microsoft 365 tools to ground every task in real business context. It can choose the best AI model for the job — including OpenAI GPT 5.5 and Anthropic models for visual work — without you lifting a finger. During its preview phase, Microsoft reported that one team compared nearly 4,000 files across two product versions, work that would have otherwise taken weeks. Microsoft also noted that Cowork became "the fastest growing feature in the history of our Frontier program."

For SMBs in South Carolina and the Southeast, this shift is significant. Cowork means your team can delegate repetitive, time-consuming workflows — spreadsheet analysis, pipeline reporting, file comparisons, content generation — and stay focused on higher-value work. Built-in checkpoints keep you in control: you can review, approve, or pause execution at any time.
