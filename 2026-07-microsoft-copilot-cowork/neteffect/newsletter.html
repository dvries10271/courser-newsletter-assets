Microsoft Copilot Cowork: Your AI Teammate That Gets Things Done

What if your team could hand off a complex, multi-step project — and come back to find it finished? That's no longer a productivity fantasy. It's what Microsoft Copilot Cowork is built to deliver, and it's available to your business right now.

Microsoft launched Copilot Cowork to general availability worldwide on June 16, 2026, for Microsoft 365 Copilot subscribers. Unlike the AI assistant your team may already use for drafting emails or summarizing meetings, Cowork represents a fundamental shift — from AI that answers questions to AI that completes work. After just three months in preview, more than half of the Fortune 500 had already adopted it, alongside organizations like Accenture, Capital Group, and Zurich Insurance.

So what can Cowork actually do for your team? According to Microsoft's official overview, you describe what you need, and Cowork sends emails, schedules meetings, creates documents, posts in Teams, and manages your calendar — with your approval before each action. It works across Word, Excel, Outlook, Teams, and SharePoint, executing long-running, multi-tool tasks and returning a completed result, not just a draft. During the preview, one team used Cowork to compare nearly 4,000 files across two product versions — work that would otherwise have taken weeks. Cowork also automatically selects the best AI model for each task, including OpenAI GPT 5.5 and Anthropic's models for visual work like PowerPoint.
