Microsoft Copilot Cowork Is Here — and It Changes Everything

What if your AI assistant didn't just answer questions — but actually finished the work? That's no longer a hypothetical. It's called Copilot Cowork, and it's now available to every Microsoft 365 Copilot user.

For years, AI tools have helped your team draft faster and search smarter. But most still left the heavy lifting to you. Microsoft Copilot Cowork changes that equation entirely. Launched to general availability worldwide on June 16, 2026, Cowork is an agentic system that plans, executes, and delivers real work — not just drafts or recommendations. You define the task; Cowork runs it end to end and hands back a completed result.

Here's what that looks like in practice. According to Microsoft's June 2026 feature release, users access Cowork through a simple Chat/Cowork toggle in the Microsoft 365 Copilot app — making it easy to shift from asking questions to delegating action. Cowork automatically selects the best AI model for each task, including GPT-5.5 Thinking for deep research and Anthropic's models for visual work like PowerPoint and graphics. It supports custom skills you can define to match your firm's workflows, plus a growing library of business plugins — including tools like Monday.com, Miro, Moodys, and more. Long-running tasks even send push notifications to your phone when complete. Powered by Work IQ, every task is grounded in your organization's existing Microsoft 365 data and systems — keeping results accurate and contextually relevant.

For South Florida SMBs juggling client deadlines, compliance requirements, and lean teams, this is a real productivity multiplier. Cowork can handle the cross-app, multi-step workflows that eat up your team's day — freeing your people to focus on billable work, client relationships, and business growth. Administrators also gain visibility and cost controls through a dedicated Cost Management Dashboard in the Microsoft 365 Admin Center.
