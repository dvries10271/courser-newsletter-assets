Meet Your New AI Coworker: Microsoft Copilot Cowork Is Here

What if you could delegate a complex, multi-step project to a capable coworker — one who never forgets a step, works across every app your team uses, and checks in before taking action? That's the promise Microsoft just made real.

On June 16, 2026, Microsoft made Copilot Cowork generally available worldwide to all Microsoft 365 Copilot subscribers. This isn't another chatbot upgrade — it marks a fundamental shift in how AI fits into your daily workflow. Where standard Copilot chat generates a draft or a recommendation, Cowork actually executes. You describe what you need, and Cowork sends emails, schedules meetings, creates documents, and posts in Teams — all with your approval before each action.

According to Microsoft's official Copilot Cowork overview, the agent is designed for complex, long-running tasks across your entire Microsoft 365 environment — the kind of work that can eat hours of your team's day. Powered by Work IQ, Cowork grounds every task in your organization's real data, so the output isn't generic — it's contextually relevant to your business. It can even automatically select the best AI model for the task at hand, and supports custom skills for specialized workflows unique to your team.

For your business, this means staff can offload repetitive, time-consuming coordination work and focus on higher-value priorities. IT administrators also get practical guardrails: a Cost Management Dashboard in the Microsoft 365 admin center lets you monitor credit usage, set spending limits, and control access — so you stay in command as adoption grows.

Cowork is live and billing is active, making now the right moment to assess how your team can put it to work. Curious where to start? Let's talk about integrating Copilot Cowork into your Microsoft 365 environment.
