Microsoft Copilot Cowork: Your Team's AI That Actually Does the Work

What if your team could hand off a complex, multi-step project — and come back to a finished deliverable? That's no longer a future promise. It's available right now, inside the Microsoft 365 tools your business already uses.

In June 2026, Microsoft officially launched Copilot Cowork as a globally available capability for Microsoft 365 Copilot license holders. Unlike the AI tools your team may already be familiar with — drafting emails, generating summaries, answering questions — Cowork operates at a fundamentally different level. It's designed to execute work from start to finish, not just assist with a single step.

According to Microsoft's official announcement, users define the task and Cowork executes it end to end, "returning a completed deliverable rather than a draft or recommendation." Powered by Work IQ, it grounds every task in your organization's real data — your files, conversations, and connected business systems. Cowork handles long-running, multi-step workflows across Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and SharePoint, while keeping human review and approval checkpoints in place. It can also automatically select the best AI model for the task at hand, choosing between GPT-5.5 Thinking for complex research and Anthropic models for visual work like PowerPoint and graphics. And with mobile support now live on iOS and Android, your team can delegate tasks on the go and return to finished outcomes.

For SMBs in Illinois, this is a meaningful shift. If your team spends hours on coordination, formatting, follow-up, and status updates across systems, Cowork is built to absorb that work — freeing your people for higher-value decisions. Security and compliance are built in too, with audit logs, eDiscovery support, and sensitivity label inheritance all included at GA.

Getting Cowork configured correctly — from admin controls to billing governance — makes all the difference in realizing its full value. That's exactly where a proactive IT partner can help.
