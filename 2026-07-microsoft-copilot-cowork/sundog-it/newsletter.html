Meet Your New AI Coworker: Microsoft Copilot Cowork Is Here

What if you could hand off a complex task — a sales report, an inbox backlog, a research brief — and come back to a finished result? That's no longer a future promise. It's what Microsoft Copilot Cowork delivers today.

Microsoft launched Copilot Cowork as a generally available feature in June 2026, marking a significant leap from AI-assisted chat to full AI-powered execution. Unlike the Copilot you may already use to summarize meetings or draft emails, Cowork is built to handle long-running, multi-step work across your Microsoft 365 apps, files, and data — autonomously, and with you in control.

Here's how it works: you describe the outcome you want, and Copilot Cowork creates a plan, reasons across your tools and files, and carries the work forward with visible checkpoints so you can steer, pause, or approve changes at any time. Powered by Work IQ, Cowork draws context from across Outlook, Teams, Excel, and the rest of Microsoft 365 — so it acts with real organizational understanding, not just generic AI. It can even automatically select the best AI model for the task, choosing between OpenAI GPT 5.5 and Anthropic models depending on the work at hand.

For SMBs already running on Microsoft 365, the practical wins are substantial. Cowork Skills let you capture repeatable workflows — your structure, your tone, your process — and apply them consistently. A mobile app for iOS and Android means you can delegate work from anywhere and return to completed deliverables. And built-in admin controls, spending limits, and a Cost Management Dashboard give your team full visibility into usage and cost.

If your team is on Microsoft 365 Business Standard or Premium, Copilot Cowork is within reach — and navigating it wisely from the start makes all the difference. We can help you evaluate readiness, configure governance, and get real value from day one.
