Microsoft Copilot Cowork: Your Team's AI That Gets Things Done

What if you could hand off a complex, multi-step task to an AI — and get back a fully completed result, not just a suggestion? That's exactly what Microsoft's newest capability promises your team.

In June 2026, Microsoft made Copilot Cowork generally available worldwide for Microsoft 365 users. Unlike standard Copilot chat — which answers questions or drafts content — Cowork is an agentic system that plans, executes, and delivers real work from start to finish. You describe the outcome you want, and Cowork runs it end-to-end across your Microsoft 365 apps, files, and data.

The difference is significant. Powered by Work IQ, Cowork draws on signals from Outlook, Teams, Excel, and the rest of your Microsoft 365 environment so it can act with context — the same understanding you'd bring to the task yourself. Users can toggle between Chat and Cowork right inside the Microsoft 365 Copilot app, choosing between answers and action depending on what the moment requires.

The early results are impressive. During its preview phase, Cowork became the fastest-growing feature in Microsoft's Frontier program, with one team using it to compare nearly 4,000 files across two product versions — work that would have otherwise taken weeks. Meanwhile, Microsoft's 2026 Work Trend Index found that 80% of AI-forward professionals report producing work they couldn't have achieved the prior year.

For your practice or business, this means fewer bottlenecks, less time lost to repetitive coordination, and more focus on the work only your team can do. But unlocking Cowork's full potential requires thoughtful setup — the right licensing, admin controls, and workflow design.
