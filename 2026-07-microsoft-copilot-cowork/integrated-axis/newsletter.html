Microsoft Copilot Cowork: Your AI Teammate That Actually Gets Things Done

What if your team could delegate an entire project to an AI — and get back a finished deliverable, not just a draft? That's no longer a future promise. It's here today.

In June 2026, Microsoft made Copilot Cowork generally available worldwide for all Microsoft 365 Copilot customers. Unlike the AI assistant you may already know — one that answers questions and drafts emails — Cowork is a fundamentally different kind of tool. It's an agentic system designed to plan, execute, and complete complex, multi-step work from start to finish, operating entirely within the Microsoft 365 apps your team already uses every day.

Here's what makes Cowork stand out. Powered by Work IQ, it draws on your organization's real data across Outlook, Teams, Excel, and more — so every task it runs reflects actual business context, not generic AI guesses. You simply describe the outcome you want, and Cowork builds a plan, runs it in the background, and checks in when it needs your approval. Microsoft has also confirmed Cowork became the fastest-growing feature in its Frontier early-access program, with more than half of the Fortune 500 already using it.
