Microsoft Copilot Cowork: Your AI That Gets Work Done

What if you could hand off a complex, multi-step business task — and come back to a finished result? That's exactly what Microsoft's latest AI breakthrough delivers, and it's available to your team right now.

In June 2026, Microsoft made Copilot Cowork generally available worldwide to all Microsoft 365 Copilot users. Unlike the Copilot features you may already use — summarizing emails, drafting documents, answering questions — Cowork takes a fundamentally different approach. It's an agentic system that plans, executes, and delivers real work from start to finish, returning a completed deliverable rather than a draft or a recommendation. You describe the outcome you want; Cowork runs it end-to-end.

Powered by Work IQ, Cowork grounds every task in the systems your business already runs on — drawing on signals across Outlook, Teams, Excel, and the rest of Microsoft 365 — so the work reflects real context. During its preview, one team used Cowork to compare nearly 4,000 files across two product versions, work that would otherwise have taken weeks. Microsoft also confirmed that Cowork became "the fastest growing feature in the history of our Frontier program." Critically for IT and operations leaders, Cowork operates within your existing Microsoft Purview security and compliance controls — and admins can set spending limits and monitor usage through a new Cost Management Dashboard right in the Microsoft 365 admin center. In July 2026, Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 5 was added to Cowork, giving users even more powerful model options for complex, multi-step work.

For your practice or business, this means real time savings on the repetitive, multi-tool tasks that slow your team down — from pipeline reviews to file comparisons to workflow coordination. You stay in control: Cowork checks in at key checkpoints, asks for clarification when needed, and never applies changes without your approval.
