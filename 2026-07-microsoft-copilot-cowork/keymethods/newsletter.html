Microsoft Copilot Cowork: Your AI That Gets Work Done

What if you could hand off a complex task to AI and come back to a finished result — not just a draft? That's exactly what Microsoft Copilot Cowork delivers, and it's now available to your team.

For years, AI tools have been great at answering questions and generating suggestions. But the real bottleneck in your workday isn't finding information — it's executing on it. Microsoft recognized this gap and built Cowork to bridge it. Copilot Cowork reached general availability worldwide in June 2026, marking a decisive shift from AI assistance to AI execution.

Here's what makes it different from the Copilot you already know. According to Microsoft, Cowork is "an agentic system that plans, executes, and delivers real work" — users define the task, and Cowork sees it through from start to finish, returning a completed deliverable. It's powered by Work IQ, which grounds every task in your organization's actual emails, meetings, files, and business systems, so the output reflects your real context. In practice, early enterprise users compared nearly four thousand files across two product versions — work that would have previously taken weeks. The Microsoft 365 Copilot app now features a simple Chat/Cowork toggle so your team can shift from answers to action in seconds. Cowork also runs on iOS and Android, so work keeps moving even when your team is away from their desks. Administrators gain a Cost Management Dashboard to monitor usage and set spending limits from day one.

For your business, this means fewer handoffs, less repetitive coordination, and more time focused on what only your team can do. Whether you're in healthcare, legal, or professional services, Cowork can handle structured, repeatable workflows — freeing your people for higher-value work.
