Microsoft Copilot Cowork: Your AI Partner That Gets Things Done

What if you could hand off a complex, multi-step task to a trusted coworker — and come back to a finished result? That's exactly the promise behind Microsoft's latest breakthrough for small and midsize businesses.

In June 2026, Microsoft launched Copilot Cowork into general availability worldwide, marking a significant shift in how AI fits into everyday work. Unlike standard Copilot chat — which answers questions and drafts content — Cowork is an agentic system that plans, executes, and delivers real, completed work. You describe the outcome you want, and Cowork takes it from start to finish.

Powered by Work IQ, Cowork draws on your existing emails, meetings, files, and Microsoft 365 data to act with genuine context — not generic responses. It selects the right AI model for the task automatically, choosing between OpenAI GPT-5.5 and Anthropic models depending on whether the work is research-heavy or design-focused. Cowork runs in the background while you stay in control, with clear checkpoints to review, approve, or redirect at any step. It's also now available on iOS and Android, so you can delegate work on the go and return to a finished outcome. For administrators, a new Cost Management Dashboard provides full visibility into usage and spending — a practical safeguard for budget-conscious teams.

For Pacific Northwest businesses in healthcare, legal, or professional services, this means your team can do more without hiring more. Routine workflows — compiling reports, managing follow-ups, generating structured documents — can be delegated confidently, freeing your people for higher-value client work. That's a real competitive edge, delivered through tools you already use every day.

Microsoft Copilot Cowork Is Now Generally Available

Microsoft Copilot Cowork reached global general availability on June 16, 2026, and is now accessible to all Microsoft 365 Copilot license holders. Unlike standard Copilot Chat, Cowork executes long-running, multi-step tasks across emails, files, meetings, and connected apps—returning a completed deliverable, not just a draft. Usage is now billed separately via Copilot Credits, with admin controls available in the Microsoft 365 admin center.
