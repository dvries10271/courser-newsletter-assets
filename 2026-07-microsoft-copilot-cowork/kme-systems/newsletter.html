What if you could hand off a complex, multi-step business task and come back to a finished result — not a draft, not a suggestion, but actual completed work? That's exactly what Microsoft's latest AI leap delivers.

Copilot Cowork changes that equation entirely. Launched to general availability worldwide in June 2026, it's an agentic system designed to plan, execute, and deliver real work — from start to finish — inside the Microsoft 365 tools your team already uses every day.
