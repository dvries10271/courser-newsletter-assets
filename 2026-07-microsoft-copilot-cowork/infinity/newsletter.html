Microsoft Copilot Cowork Is Here — and It Changes Everything

What if instead of asking AI for a draft, you could hand off the entire task — and come back to a finished deliverable? That's no longer a future promise. It's available to your team right now.

Microsoft's newest AI capability, Copilot Cowork, reached general availability worldwide in June 2026 — and it marks a meaningful shift in how businesses use AI tools. Unlike the Copilot you may already know for summarizing meetings or drafting emails, Cowork is a fully agentic system: it plans, executes, and delivers real work. You define the task, and Cowork sees it through from start to finish.

Here's what makes it different from standard AI chat. Powered by Work IQ, Cowork grounds every task in your existing Microsoft 365 data — your emails, Teams conversations, files, and calendars — so the output reflects real business context, not generic AI guesswork. You can watch it work, approve recommended actions before they're applied, and pause or redirect the task at any time. You stay in control while Cowork handles the execution.

The results from early adopters have been striking. According to Microsoft's official Cowork announcement, more than half of the Fortune 500 used Cowork during its preview period. One team compared nearly four thousand files across two product versions — work that would have taken weeks — while another used it to identify stalled sales opportunities with pinpointed follow-up recommendations. Cowork also now runs on iOS and Android, so your team can delegate tasks on the go and return to completed outcomes.
