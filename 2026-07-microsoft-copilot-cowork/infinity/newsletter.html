Microsoft Copilot Cowork Is Here — And It Changes Everything

What if you could hand off a complex, multi-step project to a trusted coworker — and come back to find it done? That's no longer a hypothetical. Microsoft Copilot Cowork is now generally available worldwide, and it's redefining what AI can actually do for your team.

For years, Copilot helped you find answers, draft emails, and summarize meetings. Cowork takes the next leap. Rather than acting as a smart assistant waiting for your next prompt, Cowork operates as an agentic system — one that plans, executes, and delivers real, completed work. You describe the outcome you want, and Cowork handles the rest from start to finish, grounded in your organization's own emails, files, meetings, and business data through its Work IQ engine.

The early results are hard to ignore. During preview, one engineering team used Cowork to compare nearly 4,000 files across two product versions — work that Microsoft noted would have otherwise taken weeks. Cowork also became "the fastest growing feature in the history of" Microsoft's Frontier program. And it's not just for tech giants: after just three months of preview, more than half of the Fortune 500 was already using it. Cowork can now automatically select the best AI model for each task — including OpenAI GPT 5.5 Thinking for deep research and Anthropic models for visual work like PowerPoint and graphics — so your team gets tailored results without having to choose a model manually.

Your team can delegate repetitive, time-consuming workflows and refocus on what only humans can do — building relationships, making judgment calls, and driving growth. Built-in admin controls, spending dashboards, and clear approval checkpoints mean you stay in the driver's seat.
