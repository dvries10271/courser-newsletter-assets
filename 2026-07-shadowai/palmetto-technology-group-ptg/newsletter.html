Shadow AI Is Already Inside Your Business — Is Your Team Ready?

Your employees are using AI tools right now — and most of them probably didn't ask for permission. That's the reality of shadow AI, and for SMBs in 2026, it's one of the fastest-growing cybersecurity risks you may not even be aware of.

Shadow AI refers to the use of consumer or third-party AI tools — think ChatGPT, Claude, or browser-based AI extensions — without the knowledge or approval of your IT team. Unlike traditional shadow IT, shadow AI goes further by involving systems that actively process, generate, and potentially retain sensitive data, creating blind spots that most organizations aren't yet equipped to govern.

The numbers behind this threat are hard to ignore. According to research cited by security experts, shadow AI adds an average of $670,000 to the cost of a data breach and takes 10 additional days to identify and contain. Meanwhile, a 2026 report from ESET found that many SMBs are significantly underestimating the risks associated with AI-enabled tools, including shadow AI — even as employee AI adoption surges. In fact, seven in ten workers now use AI tools at least a few times a week, with 80% expecting their usage to grow over the next year.

The fix isn't a blanket ban — restrictions alone often drive AI use further out of view. What your business needs is an AI Use Policy: a clear, practical framework that defines which tools are approved, how sensitive data should be handled, and what employees are expected to do before adopting a new AI service. Pair that with visibility into what tools are actually in use across your environment, and you've taken the two most important steps toward governance that works.
